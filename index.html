About the Workshop

The Theoretical Physics and Didactics Laboratory of the Faculty of Physics, under the auspices of Houari Boumediene University of Science and Technology, is opening its doors to the scientific community and enthusiasts by organizing the First National Space Science Workshop. The aim of this first national workshop is to create a unique training and exchange platform, uniting academic communities and dedicated amateurs around an integrated vision of the field. Structured around plenary lectures covering astrophysics, astronomy, cosmology, space technology, and space weather, it is designed to break down disciplinary boundaries and stimulate the emergence of interdisciplinary synergies. The workshop will include a significant practical component, with observation sessions and astrophotography training, as well as a dedicated poster session for young researchers to showcase their work. By providing a comprehensive platform combining fundamental theory, technological applications, and practical training, this event aspires to strengthen the skills of the next generation of scientists, foster innovation, and consolidate the national research network in the strategic sector of space science.

Workshop Topics

Astronomy

Data acquisition through various telescopes, multi-wavelength astronomy, astrometry, photometry, and astrophotography.

Space Weather & Solar System

Space Weather, Solar physics, solar/planetary magnetospheres, celestial mechanics, Solar system composition.

High Energy Astrophysics

Thermo-nuclear astrophysics, cosmic rays, star formation structure and evolution.

Cosmology

Universe formation and evolution, dark matter and dark energy, compact objects, gravitational waves.

Space Techniques & Data

Remote sensing, orbital mechanics, atmospheric physics, spacecraft engineering, machine learning in astronomy.
